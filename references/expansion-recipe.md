## Neckline

- V-neck modest = point above sternum
- deep-V = opens to mid-sternum, structured edge, not wardrobe fail
- Chon 1. Khong viet "or".

## Light templates (lay 1)

Studio fashion: soft key from viewer's left, slight front; wrap on near cheek and collarbones; soft contact shadow under chin; cool wall bounce opposite; no hard contrast.

Late outdoor: low sun behind or 45 degrees, gentle rim, cool open-sky fill, restrained flare.

Indoor cafe: warm practical from one side, soft window fill, shallow background.

## Camera templates (lay 1)

- Commercial 3/4: 85mm, shallow DoF, 4:5, knee-up
- Full lookbook: 85mm, full body including shoes, 3:4
- Phone story: 24-28mm equivalent, 9:16, natural proportions, no fisheye hips
- Selfie: front camera distortion mild, arm visible, face sharp

## Negative base

plastic skin, airbrushed, beauty filter, doll face, teen, childlike, extra fingers, missing fingers, deformed hands, extra limbs, warped waist, text, watermark, logo, extra jewelry unless asked
