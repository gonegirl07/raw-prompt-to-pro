# Expansion recipe

Dung khi can chon chi tiet cho mot prompt tao anh chi tiet. Khong copy so do, ten, dan toc, outfit, mau sac, hoac noi dung cua vi du vao output that neu user khong noi.

## Thu tu mo ta

Dat chi tiet co anh huong lon nhat len truoc. Mot prompt portrait thuong di theo thu tu:

`subject -> face/hair/skin -> pose/hands/gaze -> wardrobe -> textile physics -> setting -> composition -> lighting -> camera -> color/finish -> negative`

Moi lop nen co chi tiet quan sat duoc. Tranh cac tu nhu `beautiful`, `cinematic`, `luxury`, `ultra realistic` neu khong giai thich chung bang anh sang, vat lieu, mau, camera, hoac xu ly.

## Dich tinh tu tho

| User noi | Dich thanh |
|---|---|
| xinh / dep | pores, natural makeup, no beauty filter - khong lap beautiful |
| sexy | fitted cut, neckline depth, weight on one hip, fabric tension - khong lap sexy |
| cute | soft expression, rounded face cues adult, casual clothes - van 18+ |
| sang / luxury | clean wall, portrait lens suited to crop, controlled key, minimal props |
| tu nhien / candid | phone snapshot, slight noise, unposed hands |
| studio | off-white wall, key + bounce, no window chaos |
| Viet Nam | Vietnamese adult woman, black hair typical unless specified, no conical hat unless asked |

Neu tinh tu khong co quy doi chac chan, giu nguyen mood va ghi no inference thay vi tu gan mot aesthetic cu the.

## Pose va body language

Mot pose tot can tra loi duoc 6 cau:

1. Trong tam nam o chan nao, co shift hay khong?
2. Hong va vai xoay ve huong nao?
3. Hai tay dang lam gi, ngon tay dat o dau, co diem tua hay khong?
4. Dau nghieng bao nhieu, mat nhin vao dau, bieu cam gi?
5. Co tiep xuc voi tuong, ghe, ban, hoac props nao khong?
6. Pose co hop voi crop va giay/outfit trong prompt khong?

Khong dung `natural pose` mot minh. Neu user noi tay dat doc dui, giu dung y do va them chi tiet nho nhu relaxed elbows, fingers lightly extended, shoulders dropped.

## Textile physics

Mo ta theo 5 tang:

1. Chat lieu: rib-knit, jersey, denim, silk satin, linen, wool, leather, sheer mesh.
2. Cau truc: yarn thickness, knit gauge, weave direction, stitch spacing, seam and edge finish.
3. Bien dang: stretch over curves, compression, drape, fold depth, creasing, hem curl, gravity.
4. Tiep xuc: pressure points, contact shadow, overlap, occlusion, friction, transparency khi co.
5. Anh sang: matte/diffuse, satin highlight, specular ridge, roughness, shadow inside weave.

Chi them `individual fibers` khi close-up, macro, hoac light va DoF cho phep thay. Voi full-body, dung `visible weave/knit texture` de tranh chi tiet vat ly bat kha thi.

## Color va finish

Neu user khong chot mau anh, chon mot he mau nhe va ghi ro:

- palette chinh va mau background;
- white balance warm/cool hoac nhiet do mau gan dung khi no co ich;
- muc saturation va contrast;
- highlight/shadow roll-off;
- skin tone neutral, khong orange/gray;
- film grain nhe hay digital clean;
- retouching gioi han: pores va texture van con, khong plastic skin.

## Neckline

- V-neck modest = point above sternum
- deep-V = opens to mid-sternum, structured edge, not wardrobe fail
- Chon 1. Khong viet "or".

## Light templates (lay 1)

Studio fashion: soft key from viewer's left, slight front; wrap on near cheek and collarbones; soft contact shadow under chin; cool wall bounce opposite; no hard contrast.

Late outdoor: low sun behind or 45 degrees, gentle rim, cool open-sky fill, restrained flare.

Indoor cafe: warm practical from one side, soft window fill, shallow background.

## Camera templates (lay 1)

- Commercial 3/4: camera at eye level or slightly below eye level, level horizon, 85mm, shallow DoF, knee-up, 4:5.
- Full lookbook: camera near waist/chest height according to styling goal, level verticals, 50-85mm, full body including shoes, 3:4.
- Phone story: arm's-length or eye-level phone camera, 24-28mm equivalent, 9:16, mild perspective, no fisheye hips.
- Selfie: front camera at slightly above eye level, mild wide-angle distortion, arm visible, face sharp.

Neu user khong noi camera height, dung eye level cho portrait va chest/waist height cho full-body; khong tu them dramatic low angle.

## Negative base

plastic skin, airbrushed, beauty filter, doll face, teen, childlike, extra fingers, missing fingers, deformed hands, extra limbs, warped anatomy, broken joints, melted fabric, impossible weave, wrong garment construction, unwanted text, watermark, logo, extra jewelry unless asked

Neu user yeu cau chu, logo, signage, jewelry, props, tattoo, hoac watermark vi muc dich cu the, xoa muc do ra khoi negative va dua yeu cau do vao hard lock.
